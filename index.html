Level 3: Practice Check

For the grown-up: how it works
  • Each check has 40 words in 2 sections, with 4 words per page, just like the real Year 1 Phonics Screening Check. It starts with a practice page that doesn't count.
  • Words with an alien next to them are alien (made-up) words. Any sensible reading counts, e.g. jowp can rhyme with "cow" or "low".
  • Your child reads each word out loud. Sounding out first is fine. Tap ✓ if they read it correctly and ✗ if not.
  • Don't help or say the word. Tap 🔊 only if you want to hear how a word sounds.
  • The expected standard has been 32 out of 40 in every year since 2014.
